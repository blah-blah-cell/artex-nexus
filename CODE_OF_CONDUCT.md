# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and technical rigor
- Practicing responsible, authorized cybersecurity research and coordinated disclosure
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for both defenders and the broader security community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Posting undisclosed zero-days, private bug bounty details, or unauthorized target data
- Sharing malware, destructive payloads, or instructions targeting non-consenting third parties
- Harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or irresponsible behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainers via GitHub Security Advisories or direct maintainer contact. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
