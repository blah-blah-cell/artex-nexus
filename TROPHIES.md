# ARTEX-Nexus CVE & Vulnerability Trophy Case

> **Discovered a real vulnerability or got a CVE assigned using ARTEX-Nexus?**  
> We'd love to celebrate your research and credit you here!

## How to Report Your Discovery

1. **Wait for Public Disclosure / Vendor Fix First:**  
   **NEVER** post undisclosed zero-days, unpatched targets, or private bug bounty reports in public issues or pull requests. Only submit discoveries that have a **publicly disclosed advisory, vendor patch, or assigned CVE**.
2. **Submit via Issue Form or Pull Request:**
   - Open a [**CVE / Vulnerability Discovery Trophy**](https://github.com/blah-blah-cell/artex-nexus/issues/new?template=cve-trophy.yml) issue, **or**
   - Open a PR adding your row to the table below!
3. **Optional Citation in Your Writeup / Advisory:**  
   If ARTEX-Nexus helped you find or verify the issue, a shoutout link back to [`github.com/blah-blah-cell/artex-nexus`](https://github.com/blah-blah-cell/artex-nexus) helps grow the open-source research community.
